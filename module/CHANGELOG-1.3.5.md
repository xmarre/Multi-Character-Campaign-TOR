## v1.3.5
- Fixed registered TOR career companions requiring one manual player use of their career ability before AI could use it. MCC now establishes TOR's persistent `AbilityUser` prerequisite for every registered shared hero with a career before TOR's native `OnAgentCreated` handling, independent of transient `Agent.IsAIControlled` state. Existing controller-change handling still owns AI CareerAbility/WizardAI setup.
- Fixed another concrete Bannerlord 1.3.15 incompatibility in in-campaign character creation. The reconstructed NativeCreation bridge supplied `Action<object>` to `MbEvent<CharacterCreationManager>.AddNonSerializedListener`, while the runtime requires `Action<CharacterCreationManager>`; RuntimeCompatibility now adapts the listener to the exact generic delegate type before the legacy reflection call proceeds.
- Added phase-specific NativeCreation diagnostics covering wrapper entry, legacy entry, generic-listener adaptation, and escaped startup exceptions so any installation-specific creation failure identifies the actual failing layer.
- Confirmed MCC character switching does not clear TOR Waywatcher special-arrow or Runelord unit-rune troop attributes; those effects are stored on the physical party/troop extended info rather than the active career-button delegate.
- Existing saves remain compatible. No new campaign-map scan, hero/party scan, mission-tick polling, or recurring reconciliation was added.
