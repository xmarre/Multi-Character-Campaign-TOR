## v1.3.8
- Fixed TOR 1.16 `MissileCastingBehavior.UpdateTarget(Target)` crashing during mission teardown/transition states such as the hideout-to-boss handoff. The compatibility guard now short-circuits only when TOR's current target, its formation, or the incoming target has already been invalidated, returning the incoming target unchanged and leaving normal missile targeting on TOR's original path.
- Fixed additional playable characters created with a TOR culture/race different from the currently controlled hero inheriting the previous hero's body/skeleton. MCC now applies Bannerlord's already-selected `CharacterCreationContent.SelectedCulture` to the temporary active creation candidate before TOR's native `OnCultureSelected()` race/body initialization runs.
- Mixed-race shared campaigns are supported through TOR's own character-creation tables and body initialization. No Human/Dawi/Greenskin IDs or race mappings are duplicated in MCC.
- Preserved MCC's existing in-campaign creation transaction: the fix does not call Bannerlord's campaign-start `ApplyFinalEffects()`, does not replace the player-clan culture, and does not replay clan/map initialization.
- Added dedicated Bannerlord member-shape and source regression guards for the deferred culture commit and TOR missile transition repair, while retaining the v1.3.7 WizardAI inherited-agent guard, v1.3.6 NativeCreation snapshot repair, and established Harmony 2.4.x compatibility checks.
- Existing saves remain compatible. No campaign-map scans, mission-tick polling, global hero/party scans, or recurring reconciliation were added.
