## v1.3.6
- Fixed the remaining in-campaign `Create a new playable character` startup failure caused by a decompiler-corrupted NativeCreation clan-snapshot helper. The recovered helper attempted to cast clan member-name strings such as `Renown`, `Name`, and `InformalName` to `FieldInfo`, causing an exception before the character-creation state/event path could start. v1.3.6 replaces that exact corrupted helper behavior with the intended member snapshot operation and preserves independent `TextObject` copies for clan names.
- Fixed non-spellcaster registered careers such as Waywatcher missing their TOR career ability while AI-controlled or after an RTS controller switch. Registered career identity is now established from stable ownership (`registered shared hero + TOR career`) rather than transient AI-controller state or the presence of selected spells.
- Registered career heroes now receive/retain their `AbilityComponent` and `CareerAbility` even when they know no normal spells or prayers. Existing MCC AI support remains responsible for `WizardAIComponent` only while the agent is actually AI-controlled; no fake caster status, dummy lore, or placeholder spell is added.
- Preserved the v1.3.5 first-spawn `AbilityUser` prerequisite, v1.3.4 Greater Harbinger controller safety, companion dialogue activation, career-button rebinding, and existing AI career behavior for spellcasters.
- Existing saves remain compatible. No campaign-map scan, global hero/party polling, mission-tick polling, or recurring reconciliation was added.
