## v1.3.4
- Fixed in-campaign `Create a new playable character` on the Harmony 2.4 loader path reported by the v1.3.2/v1.3.3 community tester. NativeCreation now binds `Harmony` and `HarmonyMethod` through its linked `0Harmony` reference instead of the loader-sensitive assembly-qualified `Type.GetType(..., 0Harmony)` lookup.
- Replaced the recovered `CareerAbilityRepair.Install()` Harmony bootstrap with a linked-Harmony installer that reuses the existing repair callbacks without ever entering the old assembly-qualified `0Harmony` lookup. The recovered campaign/runtime repair is also installed at `OnGameStart` rather than the early `OnSubModuleLoad` phase, eliminating the reported early `CareerAbilityFix132` failure path.
- Fixed AI-controlled Necromancer `Greater Harbinger` stealing the actual player's controller/camera. Registered shared AI Necromancers and their Harbingers now remain AI-controlled while the native player-only Harbinger controller-swap behavior remains unchanged for the active player.
- Fixed companion activation being unavailable through conversation by aligning the MCC dialogue condition with Bannerlord 1.3.15's native `HeroHelper.IsCompanionInPlayerParty` predicate for unregistered companions, while retaining MCC's main-party invariant for already registered shared heroes.
- Existing saves remain compatible. No new save migration, campaign-map scan, global hero/party polling, or recurring campaign reconciliation was added.
